# AI Lead Qualification & Sales Automation

An AI-powered sales automation system built with n8n to qualify leads, manage CRM records, personalize outreach, and automate follow-ups.

## 🚀 Project Overview

This workflow automates the lead management process from initial lead capture to reply detection.

### Workflow

New Lead → Validation → Duplicate Check → Company Research → AI Qualification → Lead Scoring → CRM Update → Personalized Email → Follow-up → Reply Detection → Notification

## ✨ Key Features

- Lead capture through webhook
- Input validation
- Duplicate lead detection
- Company research
- AI-powered lead qualification
- Lead scoring from 0–100
- HOT / WARM / COLD classification
- Google Sheets CRM management
- Personalized sales email generation
- Automated follow-ups
- Lead reply detection
- CRM status updates
- Error handling and notifications

## 🧠 AI Qualification

The AI analyzes lead information and assigns:

- **HOT:** 80–100
- **WARM:** 50–79
- **COLD:** Below 50

The system also identifies buying intent, qualification reasons, and recommended next actions.
